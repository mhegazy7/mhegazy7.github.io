Company Overview

From a Mansoura rubber shop
to an automotive grade manufacturer

ICDI's manufacturing lineage runs through the Hegazy family's industrial ventures, from the first Alex Co. rubber factory in 1979 to ICDI's establishment in the 3rd Industrial Zone, 6th of October in 1997.

1979, KAFR EL BADAMAS, MANSOURA

The first Alex Co. rubber factory

Abdelmeguid Hegazy starts the Alex Co. rubber factory in Kafr El Badamas, Mansoura.

1980, MANSOURA

Expanded into a larger plant

The rubber operation moves into a larger factory to meet growing demand.

1981, BAQLIYA, MANSOURA

Mostafa Hegazy joins Alex Co.

Mostafa Hegazy joins Abdelmeguid Hegazy in Alex Co. and together they open a new factory in Baqliya, Mansoura, led by Abdelmeguid Hegazy.

1981 TO 1997

Expansion into plastic and auto feeding

Under Abdelmeguid Hegazy's leadership the business extends into plastics and the automotive feeding industry.

1997, 3RD INDUSTRIAL ZONE, 6TH OF OCTOBER

ICDI founded

International Company for Developed Industry established by Salah Hegazy, Mostafa Hegazy, and Abdelmeguid Hegazy in the 3rd Industrial Zone, 6th of October.

2007, SINBILLAWAYN, MANSOURA

Alex Co. facility upgrade

A newer, upgraded Alex Co. facility opened in Sinbillawayn, Mansoura.

SINCE

Automotive OEM partnerships

Major supply agreements secured with General Motors (Chevrolet, Opel), Ghabbour Auto (Hyundai, Chery, Mitsubishi Fuso), Kasrawy Group, Suzuki, Elsewedy Electric, and Schneider Electric, alongside continuous upgrades to manufacturing process, technology, and infrastructure.

Vision

To be the most trusted and valued partner in our community, delivering exceptional products and services that enrich people's lives.

Mission

To manufacture metal, plastic and rubber components that meet automotive quality standards at competitive cost, delivered to line sequence, and to remain the supplier our customers can build a programme around. Every process stays under one roof so accountability for tooling, quality and delivery is never divided. We remain open to new investments, partnerships and joint ventures wherever they make industrial sense.
